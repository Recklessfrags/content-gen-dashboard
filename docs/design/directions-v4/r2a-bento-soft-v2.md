DIRECTION: Editorial Bento (Soft v2 Elevated)

RATIONALE
This direction takes the approachable, modular DNA of "Bento Soft" and injects it with a high-end, editorial precision. It abandons the standard SaaS aesthetic for an opinionated mix of classic editorial typography (system serif with high contrast) against rigorous, mono-spaced data grids. The signature color is a striking, confident "Hyper-Cobalt," evoking traditional broadcast technical equipment while feeling incredibly fresh and modern. It fits a brand/money control tool by balancing extreme clarity (visible structural lines, strict bento hierarchy) with tactile warmth (heavy cardstock drop-shadows, subtle grain). The biggest risk is that the juxtaposition of elegant serif type and raw mono-data might feel too avant-garde for users expecting a conventional software template, though it perfectly answers the demand for a distinctive, characterful broadcast HQ.

TOKENS
- Palette (Light): --bg: #EAE8E4 (Warm Parchment), --surface: #FAFAFA (Crisp Card), --surface-accent: #EEF1FF, --border: #D4D1CB, --grid-line: #E2DFD9, --text: #131211 (18:1 AA), --text-dim: #63615E (5.6:1 AA), --accent: #1642E1 (6.5:1 AA), --success: #0F766E, --warn: #B45309, --danger: #C81E1E (6.1:1 AA).
- Palette (Dark): --bg: #11100F (Studio Black), --surface: #1B1A19, --surface-accent: #181C32, --border: #33312E, --grid-line: #22201E, --text: #EFEFEF (15:1 AA), --text-dim: #999794 (5.2:1 AA), --accent: #6B8AFF (6.2:1 AA), --success: #2DD4BF, --warn: #FBBF24, --danger: #F87171 (5.8:1 AA).
- Typography: Display/Titles = System Serif (Georgia, Times, ui-serif) with tight tracking. UI/Labels = System Sans-Serif. Data = System Mono (ui-monospace, SFMono).
- Scale/Spacing: Extremely high contrast. 3.5rem hero headings down to 0.75rem technical labels. Generous 2rem inner card padding.
- Radius/Elevation: 32px outer card radius, soft layered shadows simulating thick acrylic/cardstock (`0 12px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`).
- Texture: 30px background blueprint grid + coarse SVG turbulence grain overlaid at 40% multiply/overlay.
- Motion: Snappy but substantial. `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for satisfying, physical spring-lifts on hover.
